# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other MongoScope project space.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussion technical and on topic.
- Review code, not people. Explain the problem and suggest a fix.
- Help newcomers find their footing; nobody starts out knowing the codebase.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information, including connection strings or logs they shared by mistake
- Sustained disruption of threads, or repeated off-topic posting after being asked to stop

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules. Decisions consider context and history; a first minor issue usually gets a reminder.

Report a problem privately to `hello@prodios.com`, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are kept confidential.
